The 1-to-5 Repurposing Engine: 1 Video to 5 Social Networks

How top creators take one 9:16 vertical video and distribute it across Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok, X, and LinkedIn without getting flagged for duplicate content.

Key Strategy: Optimize first 3 seconds, adapt audio per platform, and leverage FeedBridge per-platform caption overrides.

1. The 9:16 Golden Aspect Ratio

Always record in 1080x1920 at 60fps. Avoid placing crucial text in the bottom 25% where Instagram and TikTok UI captions sit.

2. Native Platform Overrides

Don't use identical captions. Instagram needs 3-5 niche hashtags, YouTube Shorts needs an engaging searchable title with #Shorts, and LinkedIn needs conversational business takeaways.

Multi-User & Team Architecture: Handling Shared Social IDs Safely

2. Shared Content Calendar

All members in the workspace can inspect the interactive Content Calendar to see upcoming queued posts by color-coded platform badges, eliminating duplicate work.

3. Quota Management

FeedBridge monitors YouTube's 10,000 unit daily upload quota and shifts excess scheduled uploads automatically to the next day's reset window.
