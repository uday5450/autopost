Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 26, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. It is AutoPost's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you through our website.

1. Information We Collect

When you use AutoPost to connect and automate your posts, we access certain information required to verify and publish content on your behalf:

  • OAuth Access Tokens: Used to authenticate your accounts (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, Pinterest, TikTok) securely.
  • Basic Account Metadata: Display names, screen names, profiles pictures, and board names.
  • Post Contents: Captions, media files/URLs, and scheduled times.

2. How We Use Information

We only use your information to facilitate publishing your posts. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information or credentials with third parties.

3. Data Deletion Instructions

You can revoke access and delete your tokens or account data at any time:

  1. Log in to the AutoPost dashboard.
  2. Navigate to the Accounts section in the sidebar.
  3. Click the Disconnect or Delete button on the target account. This immediately deletes the associated tokens and posts from our database.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions about how we handle user data and personal info, feel free to contact us at support@autopost.example.com.
