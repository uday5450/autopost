Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. It is FeedBridge's policy to respect your privacy regarding any information we may collect from you through our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use FeedBridge to connect and automate your posts, we access certain information required to verify and publish content on your behalf:

  • OAuth Access Tokens: Used to authenticate your accounts (Google/YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X, Pinterest, TikTok) securely.
  • Basic Account Metadata: Display names, screen names, profile pictures, email addresses, and channel/board names.
  • Post Contents: Captions, media files/URLs, titles, categories, and scheduled publishing times.

2. How We Use Information

We only use your information to facilitate publishing your posts, display account status, and operate our service. We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information or credentials with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Sharing and Disclosure of Google User Data

We prioritize the security and privacy of Google user data. We do not share, transfer, or disclose Google user data with third parties except under the following strict conditions:

  • No Sale of Data: We do NOT sell Google user data to third parties.
  • No Sharing with Advertisers: We do NOT share Google user data with advertisers or ad networks under any circumstances.
  • Trusted Service Providers: We only share Google user data with trusted third-party service providers (such as cloud hosting infrastructure, database management services, and system analytics) that directly help operate our service.
  • Contractual Protections: These service providers are contractually required to protect user data, maintain strict confidentiality, and adhere to standard security practices.
  • Legal Disclosures: We may disclose data only if required to do so by applicable law, court order, subpoena, or valid legal process.
  • No Transfer to Data Brokers: We never transfer Google user data to data brokers, information resellers, or commercial third parties under any circumstances.

4. Google User Data Retention

We strictly limit the retention of Google user data to protect user privacy:

  • Retention Period: Google user data is retained only as long as necessary to provide the FeedBridge service to you and fulfill the functionality requested.
  • Revoking Access: Users can revoke Google access at any time directly from their Google Account Permissions settings.
  • Deletion Requests: Users can request permanent deletion of their stored data at any time via our application dashboard or by contacting support.

5. Limited Use Compliance

The use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. User Rights

You maintain full control over your personal data. As an FeedBridge user, you have the right to:

  • Request Data Deletion: Request permanent removal of all your stored personal data, credentials, and OAuth tokens.
  • Request Data Export: Request an export of the personal data associated with your account.
  • Revoke Google Access: Revoke FeedBridge's authorization to access your Google account at any time.
  • Contact Support Regarding Privacy: Contact our support team regarding any privacy questions or requests.

7. Data Deletion Instructions

You can revoke access and delete your tokens or account data at any time:

  1. Log in to the FeedBridge dashboard.
  2. Navigate to the Accounts section in the sidebar.
  3. Click the Disconnect or Delete button on the target account. This immediately deletes the associated tokens and posts from our database.
  4. Alternatively, you can send a data deletion request directly to support@growspace.solutions.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about how we handle user data and personal info, or wish to exercise any of your privacy rights, please contact us at:

Email: support@growspace.solutions
