Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about plans, billing, and the 7-day trial.

Why flat-rate pricing instead of charging per channel like Buffer? ▼
Buffer and Hootsuite charge \$6 to \$15 for every single social media account you connect. If you manage 3 Instagrams, 2 YouTubes, 4 TikToks, and a LinkedIn company page, you easily pay \$100+ to \$300/mo. FeedBridge offers a predictable flat rate: you get 15 or 99 accounts included with unlimited posts for one flat subscription.
Can I connect multiple accounts on the same platform? ▼
Yes! You can connect any mix of platforms you want. For example, on the 15-account Marketer plan, you could connect 8 Instagram accounts, 4 TikTok accounts, and 3 YouTube channels. There are no restrictions on how you allocate your accounts.
How does the 7-day free trial work? ▼
When you choose Marketer or Operator, you start with a 7-day free trial. Exactly \$0 is due today. You get immediate full access to auto-scheduling, universal composer, previews, and API/MCP. If you cancel within 7 days, you will never be charged.
What is Model Context Protocol (MCP) and how do AI agents post? ▼
Model Context Protocol (MCP) is the open standard that connects AI systems (like Claude Desktop, Gemini, Cursor, and custom AI agents) directly to tools. FeedBridge provides a native MCP server at /api/mcp/ that allows autonomous agents to fetch scheduled queues, draft captions, and trigger immediate cross-platform posts automatically.
What if I need more than 99 accounts? ▼
On the Operator plan, additional accounts cost just +₹99/mo (\$1/mo) each with no ceiling! You can also use our interactive Army of Accounts Scaler slider above to configure a custom tier with up to 250+ accounts.
Can I cancel or change plans anytime? ▼
Absolutely. There are no long-term contracts. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime with a single click from your account dashboard.
